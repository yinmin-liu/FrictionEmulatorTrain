Training-time normalization formulas:

C2_log       = ln(max(C2, 1.0e-30))
vmag_log     = ln(max(vmag, 1.0e-12))
alpha2_log   = ln(max(alpha2, 1.0e-30))

C2_norm      = (C2_log - 15.853013060672888) / 1.2777508831457713
vmag_norm    = (vmag_log - -14.369379188536289) / 1.0928669173617296
alpha2_norm  = (alpha2_log - 25.432599186435276) / 1.4568046734404581

Equivalent vmag formula:

vmag_norm    = (vmag_log + 14.369379188536289) / 1.0928669173617296

Inference-time de-normalization:

alpha2_log = alpha2_norm * 1.4568046734404581 + 25.432599186435276
alpha2     = exp(alpha2_log)
